Compliance without a decryption exception

Because detection never requires plaintext, several of the hardest controls in GDPR, HIPAA, and the NIST CSF are satisfied by construction rather than by policy.

GDPR

Art. 32 security-of-processing is met by never reconstructing personal data outside the tenant boundary.

HIPAA

PHI referenced in logs is never rendered to plaintext during monitoring, ingestion, or alerting.

NIST CSF

Detect and Protect functions are mapped to encrypted-circuit controls rather than access policy alone.
